About Me

Hi, I'm Benjamin.

I'm a student working toward a career in web development, and I learn best by building things.
Right now I'm learning Python and getting hands-on with how systems fit together. For one of my classes, I've been designing an IT system around a personal project of mine named Guardian, a personal-safety app I'd like to release someday. It would let someone trigger an alert with a safeword, stream their location to the cloud, and notify trusted contacts in an emergency.

Outside of coursework, I like working with hardware and tinkering. I've spec'd out workstation builds from the ground up, and I'm putting together a home cloud storage server from surplus parts so I don't have to rely on paid storage.

I also have a creative side: I enjoy writing, including poetry, and hope to publish my own books one day.

I'm interested in projects where technical problem-solving meets real-world usefulness, and I'm always looking for the next thing to learn..
