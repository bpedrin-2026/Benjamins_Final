Pandora's Jar


Pandora's Jar is about a project centered around the book Pandora's Lab written by Dr. Paul Offit. In his work, he explores the themes of curiosity and its consequences. His book is inspired by the Greek myth of Pandora, who opened a jar that released all the evils into the world, leaving only hope inside. Just like Pandora, science has had moments of curiosity that have led to both positive and negative consequences.

I chose this as my speech topic for my Communication class because I wanted to explore the idea of curiosity and its consequences. I wanted to understand how curiosity can lead to both positive and negative outcomes, and how it can shape our understanding of the world around us.
